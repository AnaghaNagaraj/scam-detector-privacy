Privacy Policy – E-commerce Scam Detector

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What it does. When you open a product page on a supported shopping site (Amazon, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales), the extension reads the product title, price, listed specifications, description text and visible customer reviews on that page and sends them to our analysis server to compute a scam-risk score.

Data we process. Only product-page content (title, price, specifications, description, review text/ratings). We do not collect your name, email, account details, payment information, browsing history, cookies, or content from pages outside the supported shopping sites.

Storage. Analysis requests are processed and not linked to any user identity. Server logs may keep the product URL and risk result for debugging for up to 30 days. Your settings (API address, whitelist/blacklist) are stored in your browser via Chrome storage.

Sharing. We do not sell or share data with third parties. We do not use data for advertising or credit decisions.

Compliance. The use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
